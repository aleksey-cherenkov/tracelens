ruled out:
  - http.status_code or status being 'healthy' (202/OK) proves the push messages were processed correctly 
downstream.
    http.status_code is constant at 202 across all 41 records and status is constant at 'OK' across all 273 
records — both are uninformative by construction and would look identical whether everything succeeded or 
everything silently failed.
  - A comms-orchestrator or comms-sender deploy caused the push messages to be dropped.
    The listed deploys (comms-orchestrator pr=104/sha=7d3b8e1, comms-sender 'Add SMS provider seam' 
pr=99/sha=c52a0f9, comms-sender 'Bump provider SDK' pr=101/sha=e18d773) are all noted as occurring *after* the
earliest journey in this slice, i.e. they postdate rather than precede the affected push journeys as recorded 
— that rules them out as the direct trigger for these specific journeys, though it does not rule out that the 
same underlying code path was already broken before any of these deploys.
